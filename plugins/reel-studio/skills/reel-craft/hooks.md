# Hook formulas

The hook is the first 1.5–2.5 seconds. It must create a gap the viewer needs closed.
Never open with a greeting, your name, "In this video", or context the viewer didn't ask for.

| # | Formula | Pattern | Example |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Callout + consequence | "If you X, you're Y." | "If you post daily and still don't grow, this is why." |
| 2 | Contrarian | "Stop X. Do Y." | "Stop posting every day." |
| 3 | Result first | "This got me X in Y." | "This one line got us 40 bookings in a week." |
| 4 | Mistake | "The #1 mistake X make is…" | "The #1 mistake cafés make with Instagram…" |
| 5 | Secret / insider | "Nobody tells you this about X." | "Nobody tells you this about pricing." |
| 6 | Number list | "3 things X…" | "3 words that kill your sales DMs." |
| 7 | Before / after | "I used to X. Now Y." | "I used to beg for clients. Now they DM me." |
| 8 | Question with stakes | "Why does X happen?" | "Why do 90% of reels die at 2 seconds?" |
| 9 | Direct challenge | "You're doing X wrong." | "You're writing captions backwards." |
| 10 | Story drop-in | Start mid-action | "She closed the laptop and said 'no'." |
| 11 | Visual pattern-break | Unexpected image + one line | (object smashing) "This is your budget on ads." |
| 12 | Proof stack | Specific number + timeframe | "₮12 million in 30 days. Here's the script." |
| 13 | Identity | "X people do this." | "Business owners who grow do this on Mondays." |
| 14 | Warning | "Don't X until you watch this." | "Don't hire an agency until you see this." |

Choosing:
- Educational reel → 4, 6, 8, 9
- Offer / sales reel → 3, 12, 14
- Personal brand → 7, 10, 13
- Trend / opinion → 2, 5, 11

Write 5 hook options from at least 3 formulas, score each 1–10 for curiosity × clarity × relevance to the audience, keep the best.
Specific beats vague: numbers, names, timeframes, money.
