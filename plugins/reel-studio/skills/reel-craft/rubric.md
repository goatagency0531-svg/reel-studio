# Review rubric

Score each criterion 0–10. Weighted total out of 100. **Pass = 75+ with no criterion below 5.**

| Criterion | Weight | 10 looks like |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | ×3 | Stops the scroll in ≤ 2.5 s; a clear curiosity gap; no greeting/context |
| Clarity | ×2 | One idea. A 12-year-old could repeat it. No jargon the audience doesn't use |
| Pacing | ×1.5 | Every beat 2–6 s; total within ±15% of target; no beat that could be deleted |
| Payoff | ×1.5 | Delivers what the hook promised, concretely (numbers, steps, example) |
| Brand fit | ×1 | Matches tone; none of `banned_words`; sounds like a person, not an ad |
| CTA | ×0.5 | One specific action, tied to the content |
| Visual plan | ×0.5 | Each beat's visual is shootable, varied, matches the line; ≤ 2 text cards |

Total = sum(score × weight). Max = 100.

Also check (any failure = must fix, regardless of score):
- Factual claims the brief does not support → soften or remove; never invent statistics or results.
- Health, finance, legal or before/after claims → no guarantees.
- Language: native, spoken rhythm. Read every line out loud in your head. Cut filler ("basically", "actually", "so…").
- AI-sounding phrasing → rewrite plainly (e.g. "unlock", "elevate", "in today's fast-paced world", "it's not just X, it's Y").
- Spoken numbers: write how they should be said ("forty percent", not "40%") in `voiceover`; keep digits in `on_screen`.
